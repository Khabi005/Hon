# Changelog

Notable changes to Hon. Full notes and APKs for each version are on the [Releases](https://github.com/Khabi005/Hon/releases) page.

## v0.0.4 (upcoming)

A polish and features release. The layout is unchanged; Hon now looks, moves and feels much closer to Apple Books.

- **Downloads**: download new chapters automatically, delete chapters after reading, download all remaining, range and invert selection.
- **Contents**: chapter search, All / Unread / Downloaded / Not downloaded filters, newest-first order.
- **Library**: move a novel to another source and keep your place; incognito mode; background update rules (charging only, skip finished).
- **Reader**: Audiobook Mode (text-to-speech), time left in chapter, volume-key page turns, auto-scroll, books open by zooming out of their cover, EPUB menus that match the rest of the app.
- **Look and feel**: Inter UI font, collapsing large titles, floating frosted reader controls, cover-tinted Continue card, iOS transitions and haptics.
- **Fixes**: update checks failing once history existed, taps on text not toggling controls, previous chapter jumping when scrolling up, reading time counting while idle, and more.

## v0.0.3 (2026-10-22)

- More reliable background downloads: one worker owns the download notification at a time, with clean handoffs and cleanup when downloads stop.
- Batch stops keep accurate remaining counts and never cancel a restarted download.
- Reader menus fall back to solid surfaces on older Android, in battery saver, or when blur is off.

## v0.0.2 (2026-10-22)

- **EPUB export** of downloaded novels, including chapter illustrations, with Save As and Share.
- **Chapter tools**: download next 5/10/15/25 or a custom number, multi-select, delete read or unread chapters.
- **Background update checks** with notifications.
- **Source health** screen showing what works for each source.
- Reload a chapter without losing its offline copy; back to catalog from the reader; exact chapter positioning.

## v0.0.1 (2026-10-20)

First public alpha.

- Apple Books style shelf, six reader themes and four bundled serif typefaces.
- 170+ Shosetsu novel sources with in-app sign-in.
- Background downloads with offline images.
- One library for web novels, EPUBs and PDFs, with collections and backup.
